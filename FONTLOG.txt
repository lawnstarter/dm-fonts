DM Sans, LawnStarter release

This release is a Modified Version of DM Sans under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
The Original Version is https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts, whose copyright notice the
fonts keep. The release holds every style, static and variable, as TrueType and WOFF2 files.

In the Light, Regular and Medium styles, upright and italic, the tabular digits one to nine
are the digits of DM Mono, https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-mono, under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (OFL-DMMono.txt). Each of those fonts carries the DM Mono copyright notice beside
its own.

What differs from the Original Version:

- In the six styles DM Mono draws, the tabular digits one to nine are those of DM Mono, and the
  tabular zero is the plain zero of DM Sans narrowed to the DM Mono width.
- The tabular figures feature (tnum) changes the ten digits and nothing else. The comma, the
  period, the colon, the semicolon and the space keep their proportional width.
- No glyph kerns against a tabular digit.
- The version string of each font ends with ";lawnstarter" and the release version.

With the tabular figures feature off, text is set as in the Original Version.
